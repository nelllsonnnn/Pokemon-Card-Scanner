How a move gets flagged

Each day's TCGplayer market price (roughly, what recent sales went for) is compared with the day before and the week before. A card is flagged when it moves at least — in a day or — in a week, the change is at least —, and the card is worth at least —.

One unusual sale can swing the market price of a rarely traded card, so a flagged move must also show in current listings: for a spike, the cheapest listing has to sit above last week's price; for a drop, below it. Listings priced at more than twice or less than half the market price are treated as outliers.
